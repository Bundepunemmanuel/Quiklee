Tip Calculator — Split the Bill

A $212 bill split five ways with a 20% tip is $50.88 per person — or round up to $51 flat.

Built specifically for group dinners — enter the bill and group size, and optionally round each person's share up to a clean number, so nobody's hunting for exact change.

How this works

Rounding each person's share up (rather than down) means the group slightly overpays the tip — which most servers won't mind, and it saves the group from awkward exact-change math at the table.

For an uneven split where people ordered very different amounts, this tool assumes an even split — for itemized splitting, each person's actual order needs to be tallied separately.

Frequently asked questions

How do you split a tip between people?

Add the tip to the bill for a total, then divide that total by the number of people for an even split. This calculator can also round each share up to a clean dollar amount.

Should everyone pay the same amount if orders are different?

That's a group preference, not a rule — an even split is simplest, but if orders varied a lot, it's fairer to calculate each person's tip based on what they actually ordered.

How do I use a bill splitter?

Enter the total bill, your tip percentage, and the number of people — the calculator adds the tip to the bill and divides the result evenly, with an option to round each share up to a clean amount.
