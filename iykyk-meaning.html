IYKYK Meaning

IYKYK stands for "if you know, you know" — used to reference something only a specific group will fully get, without explaining it to everyone else.

It signals an inside reference on purpose — the point is exclusivity, not confusion. Explaining the joke afterward usually defeats the purpose.

Example uses

  1. Caption on a photo from a niche event: "best night ever, iykyk" — referring to something only attendees would recognize.
  2. Comment on a regional food photo: "iykyk" — implying the dish is a local secret outsiders wouldn't know to order.
  3. Reply to an old inside joke resurfacing: "iykyk 😭" — acknowledging shared history without re-explaining it.

It's most common as a caption or comment rather than mid-sentence, and pairs naturally with an emoji rather than punctuation.
