NFT Meaning for Beginners

NFT stands for Non-Fungible Token — a way of recording on a blockchain that one specific person owns one specific digital item.

"Fungible" means interchangeable — one dollar bill is worth exactly the same as any other dollar bill. "Non-fungible" means the opposite: each one is unique and not interchangeable with another, the way an original painting isn't interchangeable with a print of it.

An NFT doesn't usually contain the actual image or file — it's a record on a blockchain (a shared, tamper-resistant public ledger) that points to a file and says who currently owns that specific token.

What people actually use them for

Mostly digital art and collectibles, but also event tickets, in-game items, and proof of membership — anything where 'exactly one specific instance of this exists, and here's who owns it' is the useful part.
