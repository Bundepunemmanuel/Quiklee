LOL Meaning — Texting vs. Work Chat

LOL stands for 'laughing out loud' — but what it signals changes a lot depending on where you see it.

In texting or DMs

Usually means genuine amusement, though often mild — most people don't literally laugh out loud when they type it. "lol yeah that's fair" is closer to a verbal shrug-smile than an actual laugh.

In Slack or work chat

Tends to soften a message rather than express amusement. "can you resend that file lol" isn't about anything funny — the LOL takes the edge off what could otherwise read as a blunt or annoyed request.

Common variants

VariantTypical meaning
lolMild amusement or softening a message
LOLSame, but capitalized for more emphasis
lolol / lolololExaggerated for comic effect, often ironic
lol.The period can read as flat or sarcastic, not genuinely amused
